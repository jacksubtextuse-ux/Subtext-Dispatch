Pittsburgh ACC Lead • Development

Pitt Trustees Greenlight 420-Bed Freshman Residence Hall at Fifth and Ruskin

First new residence hall since Nordenberg in 2013 — summer 2026 start, fall 2028 delivery — to anchor enrollment push toward 22,000 undergrads.

The University of Pittsburgh Board of Trustees has formally approved construction of a 420-bed freshman residence hall at the corner of Fifth and Ruskin avenues, advancing the institution's first new undergraduate housing asset in more than a decade. The university said work will begin in summer 2026 with delivery targeted for fall 2028.

The project is being executed by general contractor PJ Dick, with VMDO Architects and Kimmel Architecture handling design. Pitt has identified the hall as the centerpiece of a multi-phase housing strategy tied to its Plan for Pitt enrollment goal of 22,000 undergraduates on the Pittsburgh campus by 2028. University Times follow-up coverage emphasized that no new bed product has come online at Pitt since Nordenberg Hall opened in 2013, creating compounding pressure on adjacent Oakland off-campus inventory.

For private developers tracking the Oakland market, the trust-approved start date locks in a 2026–2028 construction window that will pull mid-tier private inventory absorption — modeling 2028 lease-up should assume ~420 incremental on-campus freshman beds.

Arizona State Big 12 Lead • Development

ASU Razes Three Mid-Century Dorms to Clear $400M Center Complex Site

Best, Irish and Hayden halls — built 1940, 1951 and 1956 — demolished to make way for a 2,000–2,500-bed redevelopment. First 800 beds online fall 2028.

Arizona State University has completed demolition of Best, Irish, and Hayden residential halls — three Tempe-campus dorms built between 1940 and 1956 — clearing the footprint for what ASU has positioned as its $400 million Center Complex. The replacement project is sized at roughly 2,000 to 2,500 beds and will subsume nine existing buildings across three legacy dorm compounds.

Construction is scheduled to commence fall 2026 with the first 800 beds slated to deliver in fall 2028. For Tempe operators, the net add on-campus is meaningful given the displacement of legacy beds during construction; modeling should account for short-term off-campus absorption during the 2026–2028 build window followed by a step-function on-campus supply increase at delivery.

Scion and Ares Close $910M, 12-Property Student Housing Buy from Harrison Street

Largest U.S. student housing portfolio sale of 2026 — Auburn is among the leading-university markets in the acquired book.

Harrison Street Asset Management has closed the largest U.S. student housing portfolio sale of 2026, transferring a 12-property book to affiliates of The Scion Group and an Ares Real Estate fund for $910 million. Auburn University is among the named leading-university markets represented in the portfolio. The transaction reaffirms Scion as the most active institutional accumulator of pedestrian-distance Power 4 student product and Ares as the dominant equity partner backing that buy-side strategy.

For underwriters, the trade is the first 2026 benchmark of meaningful size in the student space and is likely to be cited in cap rate and per-bed pricing discussions for the next several months. Harrison Street's exit also signals continued tactical portfolio rotation by legacy institutional holders — an inflection that buyer-side dealmakers should be tracking for the next tranche of offerings.

Wisconsin Big Ten Lead • RFP Live

Wisconsin DOA Opens Proposals for New UW-Madison Residence Hall — Responses Due July 16

UW housing operating at 115% of designed capacity; two-site RFP names Dayton/Park (Merit Hall replacement) and Mills Street as candidates.

The Wisconsin Department of Administration on May 14 issued a Request for Proposals for a new residence hall at UW-Madison, with all proposals due July 16, 2026 and a target delivery year of 2028. UW Housing is currently operating at 115% of designed capacity, with 8,987 residents housed in fall 2025 against a designed bed count of 7,749. Two specific sites have been identified for proposal evaluation: the Dayton/Park site, which would entail replacement of the existing Merit Hall, and a separate Mills Street site.

For developer-operators and capital partners, the RFP is the most actionable Big Ten P3 opportunity surfaced in 2026 to date. The 115% utilization figure is the underwriting anchor and supports demand certainty; expected partners should be focused on construction cost certainty and the trade-off between Merit Hall demolition risk and the cleaner Mills Street site.

NC State ACC Statewide Policy

Governor Stein Signs EO 36 to Address 750,000-Unit NC Housing Gap Through 2029

North Carolina Governor Josh Stein has signed Executive Order No. 36 directing cabinet agencies to expand housing supply, affordability and access statewide, citing a projected gap of more than 750,000 housing units through 2029. The order specifically directs additional grants to local governments that adopt higher-density zoning policies — a meaningful pull-through for developers seeking up-zonings in Raleigh, Durham, Chapel Hill, and Winston-Salem submarkets. For NC State, Duke, and Wake Forest pipeline players, the EO is the single most actionable statewide policy tailwind to monitor in 2026.
